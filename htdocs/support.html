Supertrainer Support

Supertrainer is a practice planner and squad health tracker for youth soccer coaches, built by MNK Solutions LLC. This page is the support contact for the iOS and Android apps.

Get help

Email support@mnksolutions.dev and we will get back to you. Include your device model and what you were doing when the problem appeared; a screenshot helps.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Every coaching feature works offline with no sign-in. An account only exists if you subscribe to Sync, which backs up your data and shares it across devices.

How do I cancel the Sync subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them from, not by us. On iPhone or iPad: Settings > your name > Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store > profile > Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops renewal; your data stays on your device either way.

How do I delete my account and data?

See Account Deletion for the exact steps. Deletion removes your synced data from our servers.

Where does my data go?

Without a Sync subscription, nowhere: it stays on the device. With Sync, data is stored on our servers in the United States. The full details, including the retention schedule for player wellness data, are in the privacy policy.

A parent got a consent or check-in email. What is it?

Coaches can send wellness check-in links by email. Before a child's first check-in, we email the parent for permission, and every later email carries a link to withdraw it. Withdrawing stops the emails and the collection.
